How OpenSpace makes money

OpenSpace is free and never takes payment in the app. When you buy something you found here, we link you to the retailer, and the retailer pays us a commission through affiliate networks such as Awin. Your price is the same.

A piece of furniture that gets returned earns us nothing — so we only make money when it actually fits. Retailer links here and in the app are affiliate links, disclosed wherever they appear.

Who is building this

OpenSpace is an independent project by Tianduo Zhai. It starts with one question people ask before every big furniture purchase: will this actually fit?

The iOS app scans a room with the iPhone Pro LiDAR sensor, measures it, and checks a real, purchasable product against the walls, walkways, doors and delivery path. It is in development now. Questions, partnerships or press: zhaitianduo@icloud.com.
